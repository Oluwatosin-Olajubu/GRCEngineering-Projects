# GRC104 Case Study: The GlobalSync Inc. Compliance Crisis

A Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) case-study submission written from the viewpoint of the newly appointed Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) of a fictional company, GlobalSync Inc. It responds to four allegations raised by a whistleblower and covers risk triage, a targeted audit, legal and regulatory analysis, and a remediation plan with a Board statement.

> **Academic exercise.** GlobalSync Inc. is a fictional company. This repository is a training submission and is not legal advice. Confirm any legal position with qualified counsel before relying on it.

## The four allegations

| ID | Allegation | Main regimes |
|----|------------|--------------|
| A | EU customer data sent to a US data centre without a lawful transfer mechanism | GDPR Chapter V; Schrems II |
| B | Brazil sales team offering "incentives" to win government contracts | FCPA; Brazil Law 12,846/2013 |
| C | No central process for data subject deletion requests | GDPR Art. 17; CCPA/CPRA; LGPD |
| D | Unable to prove that financial reporting controls are effective | Sarbanes-Oxley ss. 302, 404, 906 |

## What the document covers

## Regulatory position

The law is stated as at **26 November 2026**. Points checked against current sources include:

- The EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the pending appeal in *Latombe v Commission* (Case C-703/25 P).
- The US Department of Justice department-wide Corporate Enforcement Policy (March 2026).
- California's inflation-adjusted CCPA penalties and the limits of its private right of action.
- The status of the EU Digital Omnibus GDPR proposals (a proposal, not yet law).
- Brazil's Clean Company Act penalty regime.
